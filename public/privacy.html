2 Sensitive Permissions: Microphone & Voice Chat

Strict Ephemeral Processing Declaration:
Microphone access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO / NSMicrophoneUsageDescription) is utilized exclusively for optional, real-time spatial voice communication between players currently present in the same active game room.

Here are our non-negotiable guarantees regarding voice chat:

  • Never Recorded: We do not record, save, store, or transcript your spoken words or voice audio.
  • No Biometric Profiling: We do not extract voiceprints, acoustic signatures, or biometric identifiers.
  • Peer-Bound & Transient: Audio data is captured, compressed locally using the open-source Opus wideband codec (via TwoVoip), and relayed ephemerally in RAM strictly to verified room peers. Packets exist in memory for milliseconds and vanish immediately upon playback.
  • Muted by Default: Your microphone is strictly muted by default. You have full manual control to toggle your microphone and mute individual peers at any time.
  • Permission Optional: If you decline microphone access, you can still play the entire game, join rooms, steer your character, and hear game sound effects without restriction.

Zero-Tap Silent Authentication (Google Play Games & Apple Game Center)

To eliminate cumbersome registration forms and passwords, Hide & Play integrates native mobile gaming services:

  • Android: Utilizes Google Play Games Services v2 (GodotPlayGamesServices) to silently verify your Google Gamer profile on game launch.
  • iOS: Utilizes Apple Game Center (GameCenter / GameKit) to authenticate your account silently using your privacy-preserving Apple Team Player ID.
  • Fallback Anonymous Master UUID: If you cancel or disable platform gaming services, an AES-encrypted local UUID (user://identity.dat) is automatically generated so your gameplay, purchases, and settings remain functional without collecting any personal data.

Purchase Identity Binding & Anti-Fraud

When purchasing virtual currency (Diamonds or Coins), your pseudonymized Master UUID is passed securely to Google Play Billing (via obfuscated_account_id) and Apple StoreKit (via appAccountToken). This ensures your purchase is permanently bound to your gaming identity and can be restored at any time via the in-game Restore Purchases button without exposing financial or credit card information to SwiftUp.

4 Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures

To keep Hide & Play free-to-play for friends and families globally, we partner with Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display:

  • Ergonomic Bottom Banners: Small label banners anchored to menu footers (never interrupting active gameplay).
  • Opt-In Rewarded Videos: Videos watched voluntarily in exchange for in-game Diamonds (💎). You are never forced to watch ads to continue playing.

Google Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID)

On Android devices running API level 33+, the application declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID. Google AdMob utilizes this identifier to deliver relevant ads, prevent click fraud, and cap daily impressions.

European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Consent (GDPR / UMP)

For players in the EEA and United Kingdom, we integrate the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. On first launch, you are presented with a consent form compliant with IAB TCF v2.2. You may change or revoke your advertising consent at any time via the in-game Settings dialog by tapping "Ad Privacy & Consent".

Ad-Free Option: Players can permanently turn off all banner advertisements through the in-game Wardrobe & Store settings.

6 Data Deletion, Retention & Your Legal Rights

Under global privacy frameworks including GDPR (Articles 15–22), CCPA/CPRA, and Google Play's Data Deletion Policy, you have absolute rights over your data:

  • Right to Erase (In-App Instant Reset): Open the in-game Settings panel in the Lobby and tap "Reset Local Data". This instantly wipes all local scores, cosmetics, coins, diamonds, and identifiers from your device storage.
  • Web Data Erasure Request: You can submit a deletion request via our Data Deletion Request Portal or by emailing support@swiftup.com with your player nickname or IP address. Any server logs or feedback entries containing your details will be permanently purged within 30 days.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or disable your Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID) or iOS (Settings > Privacy > Tracking).

7 Data Security & Encryption Standards

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the game client and our relay servers use Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) and Secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • No Cloud Storage of Passwords: Because Hide & Play uses session-based OTP room codes and local saves, we never store user credentials, passwords, credit card numbers, or bank details.
