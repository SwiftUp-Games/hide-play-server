In full compliance with the Google Play User Data & Account Deletion Policy and global privacy regulations (GDPR & CCPA), this page details exactly how you can permanently erase all personal and gameplay data associated with Hide & Play.

1 Option A: Instant In-App Data Erase (Recommended)

Because Hide & Play does not mandate cloud accounts or passwords, 99% of your data resides locally on your mobile device (saved under user://save_game.json).

You can instantly delete all local data with a single tap:

  1. Open Hide & Play on your Android or iOS device.
  2. From the Main Lobby, tap the ⚙️ Settings Button located on the top-right bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Settings dialog and tap "🗑️ Reset Local Data / Clear Progress".
  4. Confirm the prompt. All stored coins, diamonds, custom outfits, player nicknames, sound preferences, and local match records are immediately and irreversibly purged from your hardware storage.
Result: Your device is instantly restored to the fresh, out-of-the-box state. No telemetry remains on your hardware.

2 Option B: Web Server Telemetry & Feedback Deletion Request

When you submit feedback using the in-game feedback box, or when you connect to online multiplayer rooms, temporary IP logs or feedback messages may be received by our relay server.

To request a complete purge of any server-side records associated with your nickname or IP address, please submit the request below or email our privacy desk:

Submit Deletion Request

Processing Timeline: In accordance with Google Play developer standards, requests submitted via email or this form are processed within 30 calendar days. You will receive an email confirmation once server logs have been permanently deleted.

3 Summary of Data Types & Retention

  • Voice Chat Audio: Stored 0 seconds. Relayed ephemerally in RAM; never recorded or saved. No deletion request needed as audio is never retained.
  • Google Advertising ID: Managed directly via Google Play Services. You can reset or delete it anytime via Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete Advertising ID.
  • Feedback Submissions: Retained up to 90 days for software stabilization and bug fixes, then permanently purged.
